32-bit RISC Processor (Mini SRC)

Digital Systems Engineering · Team of 3 · Jan — Apr 2026
  • Designed a 32-bit multi-cycle, single-bus RISC processor in Verilog with a 16-register file, HI/LO registers, 512-word memory, I/O ports and a hardwired finite-state-machine control unit (fetch, decode and up to six execute states).
  • Personally implemented the signed 32-bit non-restoring divider (quotient to LO, remainder to HI), the synchronous 512-word RAM, and the top-level CPU integration.
  • The ALU supports 13 operations, including a radix-4 Booth multiplier with bit-pair recoding that halves the partial products from 32 to 16.
  • Extended the ISA beyond the specification with immediate-operand shifts (SHRI, SHLI, SHRAI) and a hardware-managed stack (PUSH/POP using R14 as the stack pointer).
  • Built bottom-up with per-module testbenches and full regression across 32 instructions; ran the benchmark program at a CPI of 6.7 in cycle-accurate simulation (Icarus Verilog + GTKWave).
